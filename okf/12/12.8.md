# 12.8 Manage service vendors

Governing managed services, professional services, and loan staff services for IT, HR, Finance, patient care, or other.  Managing service vendors means to monitor vendor outputs against their contractual obligations and resolving discrepancies via corrective actions or monetary penalties.
