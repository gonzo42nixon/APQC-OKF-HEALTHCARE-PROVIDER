# 4.7.2 Verify insurance

Accepting certain insurances and verifying patients are covered at various levels within each insurance group. The insurance verification process is conducted in order for the provider to understand (1) the patient's coverage of certain procedures and (2) what the out of pocket expense would be for the patient should they chose to proceed with the requested care. Sometimes this process happens during the care experience when new services are necessary to treat evolving conditions.
