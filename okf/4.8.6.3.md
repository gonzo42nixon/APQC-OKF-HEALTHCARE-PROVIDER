# 4.8.6.3 Coordinate post-discharge services

Connecting patients to services that promote community living and help to delay or avoid nursing facility placement. Coordinate with social service agencies (e.g., local departments of health, social services, and community based organizations) and referring enrollees to state, local, and/or other community resources.
