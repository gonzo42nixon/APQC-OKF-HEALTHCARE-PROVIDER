# 9.2.2.3 Code the Patient's Account

Conducting concurrent coding with the episode of care, and code a patient's account after the patient is finished with the encounter and during the bill hold. Coding is where certified healthcare coders review medical documentation and apply the appropriate diagnosis, procedures and discharge codes for billing purposes.
